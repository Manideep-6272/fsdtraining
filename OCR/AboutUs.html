CVR College of Engineering

Institute History

CVR College of Engineering was established in the year 2001 by the Cherabuddi Educational Society. It was created with the core objective of intellectual engineering talent creation within Telangana. Located on a sprawling 32-acre campus at Vastunagar, Ibrahimpatnam, the institution has completed over two decades of transformative engineering services.

The college holds UGC Autonomous status, is accredited by NAAC with an 'A' grade, and has its programs consistently accredited by the National Board of Accreditation (NBA). It stands out as a leading destination for technical education owing to its exceptional faculty retention and top-tier placement records.
